Microphone and audio

EchoBack asks for the microphone when you select Start echo. Your browser holds the sound in memory for the delay, then plays it through your speaker. EchoBack sends no audio over the network.

Select Stop echo to release the microphone. You can remove microphone permission in your browser’s site settings.

Recorded takes

Nothing is recorded unless you select Record take. Saved takes stay in this browser. You can play, download or delete each take from the Takes list.

Clearing site data removes all takes stored here. It leaves any files you downloaded in your downloads folder.

Offline use

Your browser can keep a copy of the app’s pages, styles, and fonts so they load offline. Recorded takes are kept separately from those files. You can clear the copy under site data in your browser settings.
