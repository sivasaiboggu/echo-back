How it works

  1. Listen. Your mic picks up the room — the reel, the music, the video next to you.
  2. Delay. The sound waits 2 seconds in a delay line. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  3. Echo. It plays back from your speaker, two seconds behind the original. The delayed copy clashes with the original — which is the point.
  4. They turn it down. Lowering their volume kills the echo. Nobody had to argue.

Use it fairly

It only plays back what your mic hears, in the moment. Use it in shared spaces — buses, trains, waiting rooms — and switch it off once the volume drops. Don't aim it at one person for a long time. If someone asks what that sound is, tell them: it's their own audio, two seconds late.

Nothing is recorded. Live passthrough only — no recording, no storage, no uploads, no accounts, no servers. The audio never leaves this page.
