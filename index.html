    How it works

    Try the silent demo
    1. Start the echo. Allow mic access. Use headphones to avoid feedback.
    2. Find your delay. Try 0.5 s for quick phrases or 2 s to hear a little more of what you said.
    3. Listen back. Record up to 60 seconds of mic audio, then open the take to play, download or delete it.
