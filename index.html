How it works

  1. Listen. The microphone picks up sound around you.
  2. Wait. The sound passes through a short delay you can set from 0.5 to 3 seconds.
  3. Play it back. The speaker plays the delayed sound. Select Stop echo to turn the mic off.

Keep it brief in shared spaces. If someone asks what they’re hearing, tell them you’re playing the room sound back on a delay.
